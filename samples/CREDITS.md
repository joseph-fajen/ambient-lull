# Sample credits

## Piano

**Salamander Grand Piano v3** by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5 grand piano.
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

- Source: <https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3>
- These 13 notes (every 3 semitones, F♯2 to F♯5) are the MP3 versions published by the Tone.js project at <https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/>. They're unchanged here; the app mixes them to mono when it loads them.

## Upright, Harp and Vibes

From the **Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL)** by Versilian Studios, released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain; no credit required, but given gladly).

- Source: <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>
- **Upright** (soft piano): `Chordophones/Zithers/Upright Piano, Knight/Sustains`, the softer layer (`vl1_rr1`), every 4 semitones.
- **Harp:** `Chordophones/Composite Chordophones/Concert Harp`, mezzo-forte (`mf1`; `mf3` for C3), about every 3 semitones.
- **Vibes:** `Idiophones/Struck Idiophones/Vibraphone/Soft Mallets`, the softer layer (`v1`).

Converted to mono 128 kbps MP3, cut to at most 14 s with a 0.5 s fade at the end. The harp notes also had their silent starts trimmed (up to 63 ms), so they land on the beat. The VCSL files for the upright and the vibraphone are named an octave below their real pitch; here every file is named by its real pitch (A4 = 440 Hz, measured).
